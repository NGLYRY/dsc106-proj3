During final examinations, students experience varying levels of stress as indicated by their heart rates. Our study reveals two critical moments of peak stress:

  • At the beginning of the exam, when students first encounter the questions
  • Approximately 20 minutes before the end, as time pressure intensifies

This visualization tracks real-time heart rate data from multiple students, providing insights into the physiological response to academic pressure.
